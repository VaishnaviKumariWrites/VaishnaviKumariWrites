Hot takes

Things I'll argue about over momos.

Strong opinions, loosely held until someone shows me better data. Nobody has yet.

Take · on keywords

A keyword is a person with a problem.

Volume tells you how many people asked. The brief should say what was keeping them up at night.

Take · on tone

Tone is a decision, not a vibe.

A UPSC aspirant at 1 a.m. and a CISO between meetings should never get the same sentence.

Take · on publishing

Hitting publish is half-time.

Refreshes, internal links and pruning are where traffic compounds. Content you never revisit is content quietly sliding down page one.

Take · from the newsroom

If you can't say it in 20 seconds on air, you don't get it yet.

Live TV taught me to find the story fast and say it plainly. I still edit like the countdown is running.
